# AI and Fake News: Research & Interactive Web App

> A Year 9 research project investigating how generative AI and social media algorithms contribute to the creation and spread of fake news, with a special focus on its impact and challenges for older adults.

## 🌟 Features / Highlights
- **Interactive Research Presentation**: Clean, modern, glassmorphic UI displaying research questions, evidence, benefits, risks, societal impacts, and ethical considerations.
- **Accessibility & Customisation**: Includes a built-in user accessibility panel supporting font scaling (Standard/Larger), alternative reading modes (Paper/Warm/Night), and high contrast—designed with older adults in mind.
- **"Check Yourself" Toolkit**: An interactive verification checklist to help users spot potential AI-generated misinformation.
- **Project Management**: Complete chronological Research Log tracking progress from October to November.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
- **HTML5 & CSS3**: Custom responsive styling with CSS variables and glassmorphic UI effects.
- **JavaScript**: Dynamic interactivity, theme toggling, and accessibility font adjustments.

## 🔗 References & Sources
All claims and evidence are backed by reliable sources including IEEE Xplore, the European Commission, UNDP, BBC Chinese, the US Senate, and ITU reports.
